# Defmain Visual Processing & TouchDesigner Rules

## 1. Visual Choreography & Motion Rules
- **Conductor's Baton Choreography (Mandatory Default)**:
  - Unless explicitly instructed otherwise, all dynamic central visual elements (e.g., disco balls, geometry cores, orbs) MUST follow the **Conductor's Baton trajectory**:
    - **Continuous 3D Fluid Flow**: Multi-harmonic Lissajous swoop trajectories (`sphere.tx/ty/tz`) with aerodynamic wrist-banking tilt (`rx`, `rz`).
    - **4-Beat Kick Snap-Stop (The "Ictus" Groove)**: Fluid 3-beat acceleration/sweep, followed by a momentary crisp freeze/deceleration on the 4th beat (each bar's downbeat).
    - **Track-Responsive Dispersion**: Active continuous shattered explosion during drum/kick sections; smooth coalescence into a solid spinning geometry during breakdowns and ambient drops.

## 2. Lighting, Material & Contrast Standards
- **PBR Specular Integrity**: Preserve crisp metallic reflections and specular highlights without flat 2D washout.
- **Deep Contrast**: Keep perimeter backgrounds in velvety pure black (`#000000`) with diffuse, accumulating light trail ribbons and soft chromatic shadows rather than flat tinted walls.
- **Multi-Track Color Signatures**: Automatically adapt spotlight hues and diffuse trail colors based on the track's musical key (`dominant_pitch_class`), energy, and brightness.
